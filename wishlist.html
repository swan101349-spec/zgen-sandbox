Priority worklist

1. Brushes and pencils
TO BUILD · C5 + C4
XS · S · M · L · XL size buttons

Quick presets plus fine control for delicate regions. Actual stroke diameter changes, not only labels.

TO BUILD · C5
★ Favorite brushes

Tap a star to keep your best brushes together and restore favorites on return.

TO BUILD · C4
Graphite, 6B, soft blend pencil, Indian ink

Pressure-aware stroke taper, variable width, expressive trailing edge, and useful finger fallback. Confirm distinct marks on S Pen.

TO BUILD · C4
Watercolor Brush Foundry

Keep the excellent working brush and develop Round, Mop, Flat, Rigger, Sponge and Clay Roller. Community brush imports require source and license verification.

2. Three intuitive sliders
TO BUILD · C5
Top-left adjustment icon

One compact inward drawer with only three active sliders. Watercolor: Size, Water, Pigment. Pencil/ink: Size, Mark Strength, Lazy.

TO BUILD · C4
Water bleed and pigment balance

Light wash must leave an underlying drawing visible. Stronger pigment and wet spread must be genuinely adjustable and independently measurable.

TO VERIFY · C4
Advanced material settings

Opacity, edge pooling, granulation, drying, Harmony and Chaos Blender stay available without cluttering the canvas.

3. Underlays and 24-art catalog
ARCHIVE RECOVERY · FOSSICK + C5
Find the real 24 artworks

Recover original approved assets from the Basement. Make a scrollable 24-slot image catalog expandable to 48, with no invented placeholders.

TO BUILD · C2 + C5
Reference layers and personal imports

Set independent underlay transparency, position, scale and visibility. Paint translucent watercolor above without losing the tracing image.

TO BUILD · C5
Gray, dark gray and black

Three one-tap stencil tones, plus accurate placement and scaling.

4. Finish broken or missing tools
OWNER-REPORTED FAIL · C4
Real region Fill

Tap-to-fill stays inside selected boundaries, respects gaps/thresholds, and supports Undo. A button alone does not count.

PARTIAL · C1 + C4
Lazy Mode and line smoothing

Natural steady line without sluggish delay; pen pressure must retain intentional accents.

TO VERIFY · C1 + C2
History and file behavior

Undo, Redo, guarded New/Clear, Stop/Go, fit/pinch/pan/rotate, editable save/reopen, local PNG save and a separate manual Share command.

TO VERIFY · C5 + C2
Paper surfaces and tint

Cold/Hot/Rough Press, warm ivory and subtle pastel papers. Paper choice must appear consistently in the picture and exported PNG.

5. Real tests before release
C3 INDEPENDENT QA
Phone and tablet browser tests

Check 360/390/430-pixel phones and Tab S9 shapes, visible rails, first-mark timing, fill boundaries, pencil pressure fallback, imported artwork opacity, PNG pixels and saved settings.

S0 SUPERVISOR
One writer, one job, one proof

Track commits, locked engine, branch, tests, failures, rollback and next action. No unverified PASS, silent redeploy or extra hosting.

OWNER AT FINAL GATE
Only essential artistic acceptance

Owner tests OPPO/Tab S9 feel, S Pen control and brush beauty after independent QA. Release stays owner-approved.
